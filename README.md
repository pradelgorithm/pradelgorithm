## About me

I’m a **Technical Project Manager** who likes living in the sweet spot between **strategy, execution, and actually touching the code**.

I’ve worked across product, engineering, infra, backend systems, cloud, and AI-heavy workflows. I enjoy turning business chaos into technical clarity, and technical complexity into something teams can actually ship.

That usually means I’m somewhere between:

- aligning teams and unblocking delivery
- reviewing APIs, logs, pipelines, and payloads
- writing a quick script to prove a point
- debugging infra when “it should be working”
- trying to keep meetings useful and systems sane

I’m strongest when I can bridge the gap between **people, systems, and shipping**.
